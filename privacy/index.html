Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

The short version

  • Your photos never leave your device. Filmroom edits them in your browser. We never receive, upload or store them.
  • If you create an account, we keep your name, email and profile photo from Google, and the plan you have.
  • Payments are handled by Dodo Payments. We never see your card number.
  • We count visits and actions (like "chose a film look") with Google Analytics, only after you say OK. We never send photos, file names or your email to it.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.
  • You can delete your account yourself, any time.

1. Who we are

Filmroom (filmroom.in) is a website that gives your photos the look of classic film. It is run by Siddharth Simharaju ("we", "us"). For the purposes of data protection law we decide how and why your personal data is used. You can reach us at siddharth.simharaju@gmail.com.

2. Your photos

Photos you open in Filmroom are processed entirely inside your browser, on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers or to anyone else's, and we cannot see them or get them back for you. Closing the tab removes them. File names are never sent anywhere either.

4. Payments

Purchases are made through Dodo Payments, which acts as the merchant of record: it takes your payment, calculates and collects tax, and issues your invoice. You enter your card or UPI details on Dodo's page, not ours, and we never see or store them. Dodo tells us your name, email, country, payment status and payment and subscription IDs. Dodo handles your payment data under its own privacy policy.

5. Who we share data with

We use a few providers to run Filmroom. Each only gets what it needs:

  • Google: sign-in (name, email, photo, account ID); Google Fonts (your IP address, to deliver the fonts); Google Analytics (as described above).
  • Cloudflare: runs our account and billing service and stores account records.
  • GitHub: hosts the website, so it sees the usual connection details (like your IP address) when you load it.
  • Dodo Payments: payments, tax and invoices.

We do not sell your personal data, rent it, or use it for advertising. We may disclose it if the law requires, or to protect our rights and users from fraud or abuse. If Filmroom were ever sold or transferred, your account data would move with it, and we would tell you first.

These providers run servers around the world, so your data may be processed outside India (including in the United States and the EU). We use providers that apply recognised safeguards.

If you press No thanks, or your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, Filmroom does not set analytics cookies (Google Analytics is then not loaded at all for Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control). To change your choice later, clear this site's data in your browser and the notice will appear again. If you do not press OK, or you have already pressed No thanks, our server does not send Google Analytics anything about your subscription, and it forgets your analytics cookie ID.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Account and plan records: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days, or until you sign out or delete your account.
  • Analytics: up to 14 months, then Google deletes it.
  • Payment and tax records: kept by Dodo Payments for as long as the law requires, even after you delete your account.
  • Technical logs: kept briefly by our hosting providers, normally for days to weeks.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account: open Account → General → Delete account. This erases your account record and signs you out everywhere. If you have an active subscription, cancel it first (Account → Plan & billing).
  • Access, correct or export the data we hold about you: email us.
  • Withdraw consent for analytics at any time (see section 6). Withdrawing does not affect what happened before.
  • Depending on where you live (including India, the EU and the UK) you may also have the right to object to or restrict some uses, to nominate someone to exercise your rights, and to complain to your data protection authority. In India that is the Data Protection Board.

We aim to answer within 30 days. For complaints about how we handle your data, write to us first at siddharth.simharaju@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy" and we will respond as our grievance contact.

9. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Sign-in codes are random and stored only in scrambled form on our side. Payments never touch our systems. Our service checks every request on the server instead of trusting the browser. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security, but if a breach affects your data we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Children

Filmroom is for people aged 18 or over. Accounts and purchases are not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has made an account, tell us and we will delete it. Anyone can use the Free editor without an account, and then no personal data is stored by us.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your data, we will update this page and its date, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app or by email before they apply.
